When Does a Full Gearbox Replacement Make Sense?

1. Severe Metal Contamination

When an internal component (like a planetary gearset or differential pinion) disintegrates, metallic debris flows through the entire gearbox, oil pump, and cooling lines. Rebuilding an extensively contaminated unit requires hundreds of small sub-parts; replacing the entire transmission ensures no hidden grit remains to cause premature failure.

2. Cracked or Damaged Gearbox Casing

Impact damage, broken mounting lugs, or an internal shaft punching through the aluminium bellhousing typically cannot be safely repaired. A complete replacement unit provides structural integrity and exact alignment.

3. Economic Labour Practicality

On certain modern 8-speed and 9-speed automatics or complex dual-clutch units, the labour hours required to manually strip, measure, shim, and recalibrate individual components can exceed the price of an already-reconditioned, pre-tested replacement unit.

4. Minimising Vehicle Downtime

If your vehicle is essential for daily commuting or commercial business use, installing an exchange unit that is already rebuilt and tested gets you back on the road significantly faster than waiting for a multi-stage custom rebuild.

Our Step-by-Step Replacement Process

Step 1

Diagnostic Confirmation

Before unbolting anything, we conduct electronic scans and physical checks to ensure the issue is definitely the transmission and not an engine misfire, driveshaft, or wiring fault.

Step 2

Exact Code Match & Sourcing

We match the exact transmission code, gear ratios, and mechatronic software revisions to guarantee seamless compatibility with your vehicle's engine management system.

Step 3

Removal & Cooler Flush

The damaged unit is extracted. Crucially, we flush the transmission oil cooler and fluid lines to eliminate residual debris before the replacement unit is installed.

Step 4

Precision Installation & Fluid Fill

The replacement gearbox is mounted, torqued to manufacturer specifications, and filled with fresh vehicle-specific transmission fluid at the precise operating temperature.

Step 5

Software Coding & Adaptation

For modern automatics and dual-clutch units, we perform clutch bite-point adaptations and electronic synchronisation so the control modules learn shift points smoothly.

Step 6

Comprehensive Road Testing

A multi-speed road test confirms smooth gear engagement, absence of whine or vibration, and proper lock-up before the vehicle is handed over with warranty documentation.

Frequently Asked Questions About Gearbox Replacement

Will a replacement gearbox feel different to my old one?

A properly matched and adapted replacement gearbox should feel crisp, responsive, and quiet. Modern automatics may require a short learning period (approx. 50–100 miles) as the control module fine-tunes shift adaptations to your driving style.

Do I need to replace the clutch or torque converter at the same time?

On manual cars, we strongly advise inspecting and replacing the clutch and dual-mass flywheel while the gearbox is removed, as this saves substantial future labour costs. On automatics, a reconditioned or tested torque converter is fitted to prevent contaminated oil from entering the new transmission.

What warranty is provided with a replacement transmission?

Replacement gearboxes supplied and fitted by Gearbox Giants come with our standard warranty of up to 12 months or 12,000 miles (whichever occurs first). Full warranty details are clearly documented on our warranty terms page.
