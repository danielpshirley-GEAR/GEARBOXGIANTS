Understanding Repair vs. Reconditioning vs. Replacement

What Does Gearbox Reconditioning Actually Mean?

Gearbox reconditioning involves completely disassembling the transmission casing, thoroughly cleaning all internal casings and sub-assemblies, and replacing all wear components — including bearings, seals, gaskets, synchromesh rings, friction plates, and damaged gears — before reassembling to original manufacturer clearance tolerances.

When is Reconditioning the Right Choice?

When a transmission suffers widespread internal wear (such as bearing metal contamination throughout an automatic or catastrophic synchromesh failure in a manual), targeted component repair is insufficient. A full recondition restores the complete unit reliably without the excessive cost of purchasing a brand-new factory unit from a franchised main dealership.

Repair vs Reconditioning vs Replacement Comparison

  • Targeted Repair: Addresses an isolated faulty component (e.g. replacing a single mechatronic valve body or one shaft bearing) where the rest of the unit is completely healthy.
  • Full Reconditioning: Complete internal rebuild replacing all wearable items, bearings, friction materials, and seals to OEM tolerances.
  • Unit Replacement: Exchanging your faulty transmission with a pre-reconditioned matching exchange unit to minimize turnaround time.

Turnaround & Warranty Protection

Turnaround is job-dependent, typically taking between 3 to 7 working days following diagnostic assessment and parts availability. Qualifying full reconditioned units for private vehicles include our 12-Month / 12,000-Mile Warranty; commercial vehicles carry a 6-Month / 6,000-Mile Warranty.

What Determines the Reconditioning Quote?

The cost of reconditioning depends on:

  • The transmission family (Manual, Automatic, DSG, or CVT).
  • Whether internal casing housings are damaged and require specialist machining.
  • Electronic module condition (mechatronic or TCU programming requirements).
  • Availability of OEM or approved OE-equivalent component overhaul kits.

The 5-Stage Reconditioning Process

01

Vehicle Collection & Removal

Your vehicle is transported from your address to an approved engineering partner facility where the transmission is uncoupled.

02

Full Teardown & Inspection

The transmission is completely stripped down to individual gears, shafts, planetary sets, and hydraulic circuits.

03

Component Renewal

All bearings, seals, synchros, and friction plates are discarded and replaced with brand-new OEM components.

04

Precision Assembly & Shimming

Sub-assemblies are rebuilt, pre-load clearances set, and hydraulic valve bodies calibrated to exact factory tolerances.

05

Testing & Return Delivery

The vehicle undergoes comprehensive testing and adaptation calibration before being returned to your door.
