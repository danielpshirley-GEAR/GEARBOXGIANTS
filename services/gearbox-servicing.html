Important: Servicing vs. Mechanical Repair

Can an Oil Change Fix a Broken Gearbox?

We believe in complete honesty with our customers: gearbox servicing is preventative maintenance, not a cure for internal mechanical failure.

Fresh oil can help resolve minor cold-shift stiffness or sticky hydraulic valves. However, if your gearbox is already making loud grinding noises, popping out of gear, or slipping severely due to worn friction plates, changing the fluid cannot repair broken metal or restore worn clutches. In those situations, our diagnostic repair or reconditioning service is the proper solution.

Why Does Transmission Fluid Need Changing?

Busting the "Sealed for Life" Myth

Many vehicle handbooks describe automatic or manual gearboxes as "sealed for life" or "maintenance-free". However, in automotive terms, "lifetime" typically means the expected factory warranty period (often 5 to 7 years or 60,000 to 100,000 miles).

The actual manufacturers who build the gearboxes (such as ZF, Aisin Warner, and BorgWarner) recommend routine fluid changes because transmission fluid undergoes extreme thermal cycles and chemical breakdown over time.

What Happens as Fluid Degrades?

Over thousands of miles of urban driving, towing, or motorway cruising, transmission fluid gradually loses its lubricating properties:

  • Viscosity breakdown: Heat thins the oil, reducing hydraulic line pressure needed for crisp gear changes.
  • Debris accumulation: Microscopic friction material and metal particles build up, clogging fine filter screens and sticking valve body solenoids.
  • Seal hardening: Depleted additives cause internal rubber seals to harden and weep, resulting in low oil levels.

Servicing by Transmission Type

Automatic (Torque Converter)

We drain old Automatic Transmission Fluid (ATF), replace the sump pan and integrated filter screen, clean the internal pan magnets, and refill with exact manufacturer-spec ATF at regulated operating temperatures.

Dual-Clutch (DSG & Powershift)

Dual-clutch wet systems require specialised fluid to cool clutch packs and operate the mechatronic unit. We replace the fluid and high-pressure filter, then run a basic diagnostic adaptation if required.

Manual Gearboxes

Manual transmissions undergo high shear stress on gear teeth and synchroniser cones. We drain degraded gear oil, inspect for metallic debris, and refill with premium synthetic gear lubricant.
