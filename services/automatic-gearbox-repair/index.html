What to Know About Your Automatic Transmission Problem

Understanding Automatic Gearbox Operation

Traditional automatic transmissions rely on a hydraulic torque converter, planetary gearsets, and an electro-hydraulic valve body with electronic solenoids. When internal friction plates wear, hydraulic fluid degrades, or solenoid valves become fouled, drivability issues become apparent.

Can I Keep Driving?

If your automatic transmission is slipping severely (engine revs flare without vehicle acceleration), refuses to shift past 2nd/3rd gear, displays a transmission emergency message, or leaks fluid, continuing to drive risks burning out internal clutch packs. Stop somewhere safe and request a professional assessment.

Does the Entire Transmission Need Replacement?

Not always. Many automatic transmission symptoms are caused by isolated faults such as a malfunctioning mechatronic valve body, faulty shift solenoid, worn lock-up clutch inside the torque converter, or degraded transmission fluid. A proper diagnostic inspection determines whether a targeted component repair or full rebuild is warranted.

Common Symptoms of Automatic Gearbox Malfunction

Transmission Slipping & RPM Flare

The engine revs increase during acceleration or gear changes without a corresponding increase in road speed, which can indicate worn internal clutch packs or low hydraulic fluid pressure.

Delayed Drive or Reverse Engagement

A noticeable pause of several seconds when shifting from Park into Drive or Reverse, often accompanied by a sudden thud as hydraulic pressure builds.

Harsh or Jerky Gear Shifting

Aggressive knocking or jolting during upshifts or downshifts, which can be associated with sticking hydraulic valve body spools or solenoid wear.

Torque Converter Shudder at Cruising Speeds

A vibration or shuddering sensation resembling driving over rumble strips, often noticeable between 40–60 mph when the torque converter lock-up clutch attempts to engage.

Diagnostic Assessment & Repair Pathways

Through our approved engineering partner facilities, we evaluate your automatic vehicle using diagnostic telemetry, fluid condition checks, and road-load analysis:

  • Torque Converter Overhaul: Reconditioning the internal stator, one-way clutch, and lock-up friction lining.
  • Valve Body / Mechatronic Servicing: Cleaning, testing, and replacing hydraulic solenoids and electronic conductor plates.
  • Full Mechanical Rebuild: Complete teardown, replacement of all friction plates, steel plates, seals, bushings, and bearings.

Every vehicle receives a bespoke, itemised quotation based on its transmission family and diagnosed repair requirements.

Supported Automatic Transmission Families

ZF Automatic Transmissions (ZF 6HP & ZF 8HP Series)

Widely fitted across BMW (1 Series through 7 Series, X3/X5), Land Rover (Discovery, Range Rover Sport), Jaguar, and Audi models. Renowned for fast shifting, but common faults over high mileage include mechatronic bridge seal leakage, E-clutch wear, and torque converter lock-up degradation.

Mercedes-Benz 7G-Tronic & 9G-Tronic (722.9 / 725.0)

Fitted across Mercedes-Benz C-Class, E-Class, GLC, and commercial Vito/Sprinter models. Common issues include electronic conductor plate speed sensor failure and internal valve body solenoid wear.

Aisin Warner Transmissions (TF-80SC, AWF8F35, etc.)

Fitted across Volvo, Peugeot, Citroën, Vauxhall, Mini, and Toyota transverse applications. Known for smooth operation, though high-mileage units frequently suffer from valve body wear and shift flare.
