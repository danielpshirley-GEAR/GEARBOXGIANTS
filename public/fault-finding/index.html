UK

🛑 The Car Won't Move Properly

When gear selection fails, drive drops out, or the car is completely stationary:

Won't Go Into Gear →

Gear stick feels blocked, stiff, or crunches when trying to select 1st or reverse.

Goes Into Gear But Won't Move →

Engine revs in Drive or 1st, but the car remains stationary as if stuck in Neutral.

No Reverse Gear →

Forward gears work normally, but selecting Reverse fails to engage or slips.

Clutch Pedal Stuck to Floor →

Clutch pedal has zero resistance, drops to the floor mat, and cannot disengage drive.

⚡ Rough, Delayed or Erratic Shifting

When shifting feels harsh, delayed, slipping, or erratic:

Slipping in Gear →

Engine revs climb quickly on acceleration, but the car does not speed up.

DSG Juddering on Pull-Away →

Whole car shakes or shudders when moving off in 1st gear or crawling in slow traffic.

Jerking / Thumping Shifts →

Car kicks or lurches forward when changing gear or downshifting to a stop.

Delayed Engagement →

Noticeable pause before Drive or Reverse takes hold, especially on cold mornings.

Won't Shift Up →

Gearbox stays stuck in 1st/2nd or refuses to shift into higher motorway gears.

🔊 Unusual Noises & Driveline Vibrations

When acoustic sounds or vibrations change with speed or gear selection:

Whining / Humming Noise →

High-pitched siren or drone that rises and falls with road speed or acceleration.

Crunching / Grinding Shift →

Metallic grinding or crunching sensation when moving the manual gear stick.

Knocking / Clunking Noise →

Rhythmic knocking or heavy clunk when accelerating, shifting, or cornering.

Vibration at Speed →

Driveline rumble strip shudder or cabin vibration at 50–70 mph under throttle.

⚠️ Warning Lights & Electronics

When electronic alerts appear on the instrument cluster:

Gearbox Warning Light →

Amber cog symbol, exclamation mark, or 'Transmission Fault' alert on dashboard.

Gearbox Limp Mode →

Transmission restricts operating range to protect internal components from damage.

Flashing PRNDS / Spanner →

Flashing gear indicator on dual-clutch cars indicating TCU or mechatronic faults.

💧 Leaks, Shifter Issues & Overheating

When physical fluid, mechanical linkage wear, or thermal warnings occur:

Gearbox Oil Leak →

Red, amber, or brown oily fluid dripping underneath the front or centre of the car.

Loose or Floppy Gear Stick →

Gear lever feels sloppy, spins freely, or has disconnected from the gearbox linkages.

Transmission Overheating →

High transmission temperature alert or acrid burning oil odour requiring safe stop.

CVT Transmission Shuddering →

Take-off hesitation, RPM hunting, or shudder on continuously variable transmissions.
