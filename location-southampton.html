Frequently Asked Questions — Southampton Collections

Can you collect from Southampton Docks or marine industrial estates?

Yes. Collections can be arranged from commercial freight hubs, dockside workplaces, and private addresses across the SO postal area.

What should I do if the vehicle is in transmission emergency mode?

Do not attempt to drive on the motorway. Note any error messages and contact our team so flatbed collection can be scheduled safely.

Where are the transmission engineering partner cleanrooms located?

Repairs take place in specialized engineering partner facilities equipped with diagnostic rigs and dynamometers. Once verified, the vehicle is returned to Southampton.
