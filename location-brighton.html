Steep Gradient Driving & Coastal Arterials

Brighton's topography features severe urban gradients (such as Elm Grove, Bear Road, and Hanover streets) combined with dense 20mph traffic. Constant hill-starts place intense thermal stress on manual clutches, dual-mass flywheels, and DSG clutch pack friction linings.

Recovery operations utilize the A23 (London Road) arterial leading directly to the M23 corridor, the A27 South Coast bypass connecting Worthing and Lewes, and the coastal A259 route.

Hills & Slipping Transmissions: If your vehicle is struggling on Brighton inclines or slipping under load, arranged flatbed collection prevents clutch burnout or total transmission lockup.

Frequently Asked Questions — Brighton Collections

Can you collect from narrow residential streets in Hanover or Kemptown?

Yes. Our recovery partners use suitable vehicle transporters experienced with Brighton's narrow terraced streets and parking restrictions.

My car is slipping severely on hills. Should I keep driving it?

We strongly recommend avoiding driving a slipping transmission up steep inclines, as this quickly overheats clutch friction plates and damages flywheel friction faces. Arranged collection prevents further secondary damage.

Where is the gearbox reconditioning conducted?

Rebuilds are carried out in dedicated partner engineering cleanrooms. Once road-tested and quality-checked, your vehicle is returned to Brighton.

How quickly can vehicle collection take place?

Collection is typically booked within 24 to 48 hours following quote approval and customer scheduling.
