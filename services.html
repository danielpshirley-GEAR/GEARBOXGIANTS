COMMON SYMPTOMS

SIGNS YOUR GEARBOX NEEDS IMMEDIATE ATTENTION

If you experience any of these symptoms contact us as soon as possible, as continued driving can cause irreversible transmission damage.

Grinding or shaking when changing gears
Indicates synchromesh ring wear or clutch disengagement failure.
Delayed or slipping gear shifts
Engine revs flare up without acceleration or delayed automatic gear kickdown.
Unusual noises (whining, clunking, humming)
High pitched whine from bearings or heavy clunking during gear engagement.
Burning smell from transmission fluid
Fluid overheating due to friction breakdown, loss of lubrication, or clutch friction burn.
Leaking transmission fluid under the vehicle
Red/brown puddles on your driveway indicating cracked casing, axle seal or pan breach.
Dashboard warning light (Transmission / Engine)
TCM detects internal electronic solenoid or mechanical gear ratio fault.
Poor acceleration or lack of power
Gearbox enters limp mode or fails to deliver engine torque efficiently to drive wheels.
Gears not engaging or difficulty getting into gear
Complete lock-out of specific gears (e.g. Reverse or 1st gear) or selector fork jam.
Excessive transmission noise when in neutral
Worn input shaft bearings that rattle while idling in neutral and disappear when clutch depressed.
Jerking or surging during driving
Transmission shifts irregularly or jerks aggressively between lower gears.
