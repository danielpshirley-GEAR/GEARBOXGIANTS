Frequently Asked Questions — Harrow Collections

Can you collect my vehicle from Harrow?

Yes. Vehicle collection can be arranged across Harrow and surrounding HA postcodes, subject to scheduling and quote confirmation.

What should I do if the vehicle cannot move?

If your car is in limp mode, has lost gear engagement, or produces heavy grinding noises, inform our team when requesting a quote so that appropriate transporter collection can be arranged.

Where does the gearbox repair take place?

Repairs are carried out at dedicated specialist engineering partner facilities equipped for transmission diagnostics, teardown, and rebuilding. Once tested, your vehicle is returned to Harrow.
