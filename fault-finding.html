ENGINEERING PROTOCOL

STEP-BY-STEP MECHANICAL GUIDE

How our specialist technicians systematically isolate, test, and pinpoint transmission component failures.

01 Acoustic

Unusual Sounds & Whining

Electronic stethoscope listening identifies high-pitched whining from bearing races, metallic crunching from synchromesh wear, or low-frequency humming from differential pinions.

Inspection Target
Input Bearings & Ring Gears
02 Shifting

Difficulty Shifting Gears

Evaluates mechanical resistance, synchro ring taper angles, and selector fork alignment when shifting into 1st, 2nd, or Reverse under engine load.

Inspection Target
Synchro Cones & Selector Forks
03 OBD-II

Dashboard Warning Lights

Live OBD-II scanning interrogates the Transmission Control Module for stored DTC codes (such as P177B, P0700), clutch stroke sensor tolerances, and solenoid duty cycles.

Inspection Target
Mechatronic TCM & Sensors
04 Hydraulics

Delayed Gear Engagement

Pressure gauge tapping verifies line pressure build-up when shifting from Park into Drive/Reverse. Measures valve body solenoid response and torque converter stator lockup.

Inspection Target
Valve Body Line Pressure
05 Seals & Oil

Visual Inspection & Fluid Leaks

Optical fluid analysis tests for metallic particle contamination, burnt clutch odor, and degraded viscosity. Housing inspection checks output shaft seals and bellhousing gaskets.

Inspection Target
ATF Fluid & Shaft Seals
