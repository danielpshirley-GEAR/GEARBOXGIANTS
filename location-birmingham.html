Our Step-by-Step Transmission Service Process

01

VRM & Symptom Assessment

Submit your vehicle registration and outline symptoms (e.g. erratic shifts, limp mode alerts, grinding noises, or fluid leaks).

02

Bespoke Repair Estimation

We review exact gearbox codes and provide an itemised breakdown covering diagnostic inspection, parts, and rebuilding options.

03

Birmingham Area Collection

A vehicle transporter collects your vehicle from your nominated home, business, or repair garage in Birmingham.

04

Specialist Teardown & Overhaul

Specialist transmission engineers strip the unit, replace worn planetary gears, clutch packs, or mechatronic units, and rebuild to OEM standards.

05

Verification & Return Delivery

Following calibration and road testing, your vehicle is transported back to your Birmingham address with full warranty terms.
