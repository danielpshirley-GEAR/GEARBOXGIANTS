Frequently Asked Questions — Guildford Collections

Can you collect from Surrey Research Park or local business addresses?

Yes. Vehicle collection can be arranged from corporate sites, business parks, residential driveways, or independent garages throughout the GU postcode area.

Can you repair ZF 8-speed automatic gearboxes fitted to BMW, Jaguar, and Land Rover?

Yes. We regularly diagnose and rebuild ZF 8HP gearboxes, addressing mechatronic bridge seal leaks, clutch friction wear, and torque converter lockup shudder.

Where is the transmission overhaul carried out?

Work is completed in dedicated partner engineering facilities with cleanroom rebuild bays. After calibration and road testing, your car is returned to Guildford.

How quickly can vehicle recovery be scheduled?

Collection is typically booked within 24 to 48 hours following quotation acceptance and scheduling confirmation.
