Frequently Asked Questions — Croydon Collections

Can you pick up my car from anywhere in Croydon?

Yes. We arrange collections from private driveways, workplace car parks, and independent garages across all CR postcodes.

My car is completely stuck in park / neutral — can it be collected?

Yes. Our transporter logistics accommodate immobilised vehicles using winch-equipped transporters, ensuring safe loading without stressing the driveline.

Where are the transmission repairs carried out?

Work is completed at dedicated partner engineering facilities with specialised gearbox cleanrooms and diagnostic test benches. After testing, your car is returned directly to Croydon.
