🛑 The Car Won't Move Properly

When gear selection fails, drive drops out, or the car is completely stationary:

Won't Go Into Gear →

Gear stick feels blocked, stiff, or crunches when trying to select 1st or reverse.

Goes Into Gear But Won't Move →

Engine revs in Drive or 1st, but the car remains stationary as if stuck in Neutral.

No Reverse Gear →

Forward gears work normally, but selecting Reverse does nothing or slips freely.

Losing Drive While Driving →

Drive suddenly disengages at speed, causing revs to flare with zero acceleration.

⚡ Rough or Unusual Gear Changes

When shifting feels harsh, delayed, slipping, or erratic:

Slipping in Gear →

Engine revs climb quickly on hills or acceleration, but the car doesn't speed up.

Juddering on Pull-Away →

Whole car shakes or shudders when moving off in 1st gear or crawling in slow traffic.

Jerking / Thumping Shifts →

Car kicks or lurches forward when changing gear or downshifting to a stop.

Delayed Engagement →

Noticeable pause before Drive or Reverse engages, especially when cold in the morning.

🔊 Unusual Drivetrain Noises

When acoustic sounds change with speed or gear selection:

Whining / Humming Noise →

High-pitched siren or drone that rises and falls with road speed or acceleration.

Grinding When Changing Gear →

Metallic crunching when moving the gear stick into a specific gear.

Rattling / Clattering at Idle →

Rattle in neutral that quiets down when pressing the clutch pedal (flywheel wear).

⚠️ Warning Lights & Messages

When electronic alerts appear on the dashboard:

Gearbox Warning Light →

Amber cog symbol, exclamation mark, or 'Transmission Fault' alert on dashboard.

Gearbox Limp Mode →

Car loses acceleration and locks in 3rd gear as an emergency protective measure.

Flashing PRNDS / Spanner Icon →

Flashing gear indicator on dual-clutch cars indicating mechatronic pressure drops.

💧 Leaks, Smells & Overheating

When physical fluid, smells, or thermal warnings occur:

Transmission Fluid Leak →

Red, amber, or brown oily puddles underneath the center or front of the car.

Burning Friction Smell →

Acrid burning odour from engine bay caused by slipping clutch friction discs.

Transmission Overheating Warning →

High transmission oil temperature alert requiring safe pull-over.
